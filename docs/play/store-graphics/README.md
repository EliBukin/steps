# Store graphics for Steps GPS Route

Made 2026-10-21 for the Play store listing. Nothing has been uploaded.

## What to upload where (Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing)

| Play field | File | Spec (Google, checked 2026-10-21) | This file |
|---|---|---|---|
| App icon | `icon-512.png` | 512 × 512, 32-bit PNG with alpha, ≤ 1 MB | 512 × 512 RGBA, 206 KB |
| Feature graphic | `feature-graphic-1024x500.png` | 1024 × 500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha | 1024 × 500 RGB, 100 KB |
| Phone screenshots, English (en-US) | `screenshots/en/01…08` | 2-8; JPEG or 24-bit PNG; sides 320-3840 px; long side ≤ 2× short side. To be eligible for promotion: at least 4, portrait 9:16, at least 1080 × 1920. | 8 × 1080 × 1920 RGB (9:16) |
| Phone screenshots, Hebrew (iw-IL) | `screenshots/he/01…04` | the same | 4 × 1080 × 1920 RGB |

Upload in the numbered order: Play shows the first ones first.

**English:**

1. `01-trip-map.png`: a hike on the map, colored by speed, with four numbered spots.
2. `02-trip-details.png`: the details panel with distance, duration, moving time, climb, top speed
   and the elevation chart.
3. `03-today.png`: today's steps against the goal, workout and incidental steps, and the week's
   total.
4. `04-trips-recording.png`: a trip recording (GPS: Good, Mark spot, Finish trip), with "Spot 1
   marked".
5. `05-share-gpx.png`: Android's share sheet for the GPX file, with the app's own *Save file*
   first.
6. `06-history.png`: the last 7 days as bars against the goal.
7. `07-stats-steps.png`: lifetime step stats.
8. `08-stats-trips.png`: Stats for GPS trips, walks and drives.

**Hebrew:** the trip map (the Arad to Dead Sea drive), the details panel, Today and a trip
recording. Their trip names are English: the demo data has English names.

**Spare:** `extra/trips-list.png`, the Trips screen when not recording. Swap it in for one of the
eight if preferred.

## How they were made

- **App:** the debug build of main `dc88b27` (`com.example.stepsplit.debug`), with no code changes.
  The release build looks the same, except that Settings has no *Debug tools*; no screenshot shows
  Settings.
- **Device:** a disposable Android 36 emulator (Google APIs, x86_64) on port 5556.
  - Screen: 1080 × 1920 at **380 dpi**, which is 455 × 808 dp, close to a typical phone. At the
    default 420 dpi (411 × 731 dp), the trip details panel and its elevation chart didn't fit on
    the 9:16 screen.
  - GPU mode `-gpu swangle`, so the map draws its labels and spot numbers.
  - The AVD was deleted after the capture. The owner's phones were not touched.
- **Data:** all made up, never the owner's.
  - The trips are the synthetic trips seeded earlier on a test emulator: *Sataf and Ein Kerem
    hills*, *Arad down to the Dead Sea* and others, with made-up named spots.
  - Ten weeks of steps were generated by a script from the earlier README-screenshot session
    (`make_demo_db.py`, kept outside the repo), ending on Tue 20/10/2026, with the emulator clock
    set to 18:40 Israel time.
  - The recording screenshots come from a short walk fed to the emulator as fake GPS fixes.
- **Status bar:** Android's demo mode: clock 6:40, full battery and Wi-Fi, no notification icons,
  no mobile data label.
- **Map:** live OpenFreeMap tiles, which hold OpenStreetMap data. The map shows MapLibre's logo and
  its attribution (i) button, as the app always does.
- **Packaging:** `tools/package_screenshots.py` converts each capture to 24-bit RGB PNG and checks
  that no pixel changed. Nothing is cropped, retouched, framed or captioned.

## Icon

`tools/make_icon.py` builds the icon from the launcher icon, with no redesign:

- the adaptive icon's background color (`#0F213E`) plus the xxxhdpi foreground PNG;
- cut to the central 72 dp that launchers show;
- scaled to 512 px. Play adds its own rounded corners.

⚠ The largest copy of the artwork in the repo is 432 px (288 px visible), so the icon is upscaled
1.78×. It looks fine at store sizes. If the original artwork exists at 1024 px or more, export the
icon from that instead.

## Feature graphic

`tools/make_feature_graphic.py` draws an illustration, not app UI:

- the icon's navy fading into the app's green;
- a route in the trip map's speed colors (red, yellow, green), with a green start, a red finish and
  two numbered spots in the map's marker style;
- a partial ring like the Today screen's;
- the app name in large type.

Following Google's guidance, it avoids:

- small text;
- device frames, store badges and claims ("Best", "#1", "New", …);
- a white, black or dark gray background;
- a copy of the app icon.

The important elements stay away from the edges.

## Things the owner may want to change

- **`05-share-gpx.png`** shows Google's Quick Share, Gmail and Drive icons in the system share
  sheet. That's normal for a screenshot of Android's own UI. If you'd rather not show other
  products' icons, swap in `extra/trips-list.png`.
- **The map screenshots show OpenStreetMap data.** The credit is behind the map's (i) button, as in
  the app. Adding "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors" to the full description would be a
  courteous extra. See the suggestions in `../PUBLICATION-CHECKLIST.md`.
- **Re-shoot** if the release UI changes visibly, for example the Open map work.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 -I docs/play/store-graphics/tools/make_icon.py . docs/play/store-graphics/icon-512.png
python3 -I docs/play/store-graphics/tools/make_feature_graphic.py docs/play/store-graphics/feature-graphic-1024x500.png
python3 -I docs/play/store-graphics/tools/package_screenshots.py RAW_DIR OUT_DIR raw-name=out-name ...
```

Pillow is needed. The feature graphic takes about 45 seconds: it's drawn at 4× and scaled down.
