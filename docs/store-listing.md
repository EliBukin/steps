**Full description** (2,181 of 4,000 characters)

```text
Steps GPS Route does two simple things. It shows how much you walk each day, and it records the GPS route of trips you start yourself, such as a hike, a nature walk or an off-road drive, so you can see them on a map and share them as GPX files.

Steps
• Today's steps in a ring that fills toward your daily goal, with this week's total
• The last 7 days as a chart, split into workout walks and everyday steps
• Lifetime numbers: total steps, best day, active days, and a few just for fun, like your progress around the Earth
• Steps come from Health Connect (Android 14 or newer). The app only reads them, and only while it's open.

GPS trips
• Start a trip with one tap. Recording continues with the screen off.
• Mark a spot, such as a spring, a viewpoint or a tricky passage, from the app, the notification or the lock screen
• See each trip on a map, colored by speed, with the spots you marked
• Trip details: distance, duration, moving time, top speed, climb and descent, and a height chart
• Name a trip and set it as a walk or a drive. Stats counts walks and drives separately.
• Keeps recording at driving speed, for 4x4 and jeep trips

GPX for other apps
• Share a trip as a GPX file with any app, or save it to a folder you choose
• The file holds the route with times and heights, the trip's name, and your spots as waypoints

Your data stays on your phone
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Nothing is sent to the developer
• Location is used only while a trip you started is recording
• The only internet use is loading the map (from OpenFreeMap) on a trip's screen
• Privacy policy: https://elibukin.github.io/steps/privacy-policy.html

Not a health app
Steps GPS Route is not a medical, health or fitness-coaching app, and it gives no health advice. Calories, "burgers burned" and similar numbers are rough estimates for fun, not measurements.

Good to know
• Trips work on Android 8.0 and newer. Step counting needs Android 14 or newer with Health Connect, and something that saves your steps to it, such as your phone itself or your watch's app.
• Android's battery saver can stop a long recording. If it does, you can resume the trip.
• In English and Hebrew.
```

## Notes for the owner

- The privacy policy page is in English only. A Hebrew listing may link to it as it is.
- "Available in Hebrew" is true for any phone not set to English: the app shows Hebrew for every
  language except English.
- The listing also needs a 512 x 512 icon, a 1024 x 500 feature graphic and 2-8 phone screenshots.
  The README's screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` are 540 px wide. Play accepts that, but
  recommends at least 1080 px, so take fresh full-size captures for Play (the same demo data can
  be reused).
